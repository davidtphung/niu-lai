    How it was made

    Production

    Crew

    Two people

    Xin Yumeng and Sun Lifang

    A son and his mother. He animated. She wrote, scored, and acted with him. No studio staff.

    Time

    Five years

    2021 to 2026

    Started in the Year of the Ox. Characters took three to four months. The rest was handmade animation.

    Cash

    Production cost

    Several thousand yuan

    About $200 to $7,000

    Wikipedia says several thousand yuan. English recaps often cite $200. Some uploads say about $7,000. No outside backers. Pick a currency to convert both figures.

    Tools

    SketchUp

    Second-hand hardware, no AI

    Architectural software instead of a studio pipeline. New computers were too expensive, so they bought used parts online.

    Company

    Interior design first

    Registered as media in July 2021

    Dalian Jingyuan began as a decoration firm. Tianjin Yamazaki still released the film after being told not to.

    What happened

    Timeline

    1. 2021

      Xin Yumeng starts the film. Sun Lifang writes, scores, and acts with him.

    2. 25 Aug 2025

      China public screening license is issued. No trailer campaign follows.

    3. 5 Aug 2026

      Opens in 245 theatres. Opening day: 122 people, 16 refunds, 3,420 yuan.

    4. 14 Aug 2026

      236 tickets and 7,169 yuan after nine days. Down to four theatres.

    5. 15 Aug 2026

      Mockery turns into demand. Daily screenings jump from 21 to 359, then toward 2,500.

    6. 16 Aug 2026

      157,000 tickets by mid-afternoon. A full-film copy lands on YouTube.

    7. 18 Aug 2026

      Maoyan: 639,000 tickets and 17.63 million yuan. A TikTok explainer passes 1.2 million views.

    8. 22 Aug 2026

      Reported box office 41.31 million yuan. This tracker stays on the public counts.
